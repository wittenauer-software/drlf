# Community conduct

DRLF participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, protect privacy,
avoid harassment or discrimination, and keep identifiable healthcare allegations out of project
surfaces. Examples of unacceptable behavior include threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory or
demeaning comments, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, lock, or reject material and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently exclude participants in proportion to the behavior and platform capabilities. They should
record moderation decisions privately and disclose conflicts.

For a routine, non-sensitive policy concern, flag the content using GitHub's built-in reporting tools
without quoting sensitive material. For behavior that violates GitHub's Community Guidelines or
Terms of Service, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
That route goes to GitHub and is the independent escalation when a concern involves a DRLF maintainer
or a maintainer has a conflict. GitHub decides matters within its own platform policies; it does not
act as DRLF's moderator or promise to resolve every project disagreement.

DRLF has no separate confidential maintainer conduct inbox in v0.1. Do not put sensitive conduct
details in public project posts or send them to an inferred personal address. A later confidential
project route must be access-reviewed and receipt-tested before this policy advertises it.

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents DRLF. It does not create
an investigative channel for healthcare claims or fraud tips.
